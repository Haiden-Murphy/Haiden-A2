Experience 1: Karate


What was it that you did?

I did karate; I still do, and I've been doing it for about a decade now.


When did you do it?

I started back in 2017, I believe, and I'm still doing it. There's class every 
Tuesday and Thursday, but recently I've only been going on Thursdays because of school.


What was good about it?

Knowing how to defend yourself, even with a basic understanding, could save your life. 
I feel it's a crucial skill. It doesn't necessarily have to be picked up from karate, though; 
boxing, kickboxing, wrestling, judo, and more are all completely acceptable. It's also good 
exercise and keeps you in shape.


What was your favourite memory of it?

Achieving my first degree black belt. I remember training and practicing for the grading with 
my sister, even going to the pre-grading (a practice test). The actual grading took 6 hours; 
they run you through everything: basics, katas (combinations/patterns and different techniques 
combined to defend and fight off multiple imaginary attackers), sparring (with other students 
being graded), all while judges watch you. Needless to say, my sister and I were gassed at the 
end, but we were awarded our belts. Best feeling.


What didn't you enjoy about it?

Sometimes, especially when my sister and I were younger and only a couple of years into Karate, 
we began to feel bored and didn't feel like going. It became stressful and annoying at times trying 
to balance it with school. My grandparents forced us to go anyway. Luckily, they did, even though 
it felt like a drag at times.


Give your experience a letter "grade"

A-B: Enjoyable for the most part, though there are times when I feel like it's a lot.
