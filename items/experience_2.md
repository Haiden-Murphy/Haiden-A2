Experience 2: IBM Internship

What was it that you did?
The highschool I attended did a co-op program with mohawk college. Through this I took a few programming courses and was given the oportunity to work as an intern with IBM for two summers. It was remote work, though, I did have to show up at my highschool during the intended work hours. For the first internship I was in a group with four other class mates who got to work with a client. Then the second summer I was apart of a much larger group with my classmates and other interns from different schools.

When did you do it?
I did this during my last two years of highschool. That would have been 2020-2021 I think.

What was good about it?
The main thing is that it looks great on a resume. But, it also gave me exposure to some of the inner workings of the field I might be going into in the future. That being one where I'm part of a team, designing and coding in different software.

What was your favourite memory of it?
Helping with the design portion that each job offered.

What didn't you enjoy about it?
Having to walk all the way to my highschool which was a 45min walk. This was when I didnt yet have a car or my full license, it was rough.
