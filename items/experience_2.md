Experience 2: IBM Internship


What was it that you did?

My high school did a co-op program with Mohawk College. Through this, I took a few programming 
courses and was given the opportunity to work as a paid intern with IBM for two summers. It was 
remote work, though; I still had to show up at my high school during the intended work hours. 
For the first internship, I was in a group with four other classmates who worked with a client. 
Then, the second summer I was a part of a much larger group with my classmates and other interns 
from different schools.


When did you do it?

I did this during my last two years of high school. That would have been 2020-2021, I think.


What was good about it?

The main thing is that it looks great on a resume. It also gave me exposure to some of the inner 
workings of the field I might go into in the future. That being one where I'm part of a team, 
designing and coding in different software.


What was your favourite memory of it?

Helping with the design portion that each job offered. I was usually on the front end and helped 
with related decision-making. Got to put some of my art skills to use. Also, we used HTML and 
CSS, which was nice.


What didn't you enjoy about it?

Having to walk all the way to my high school, which was a 45-minute walk. This was before I had 
a car or my full license; it was rough.


Give your experience a letter "grade"

B
