## Experience 3: Toyota Summer Student

### What was it that you did?
I have worked at Toyota each summer for the past three years. Not at a car dealership, 
but at the manufacturing plant. I worked at both the Woodstock and Cambridge plants. I 
worked multiple jobs on a moving line. Cars moved down, and I installed different parts 
before they left my area.

### When did you do it?
I did this last summer and the previous two summers, for a total of three summers. I'm 
going into my fourth this coming summer, since I have already submitted my application.

### What was good about it?
The money was pretty much the only good thing about it, and the experience, I'll be honest. 
It's definitely hard work and physically demanding. My co-workers who have been there much 
longer talk about the tax it takes on their bodies.

### What was your favourite memory of it?
Making friends, getting to know the people I work with and understanding the workplace 
culture. 

### What didn't you enjoy about it?
The physical demands, the unstructured summer student program, and the stress. But that's 
pretty much it.

### Give your experience a letter "grade"
C
