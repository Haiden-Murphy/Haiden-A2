## Experience 4: Drawing and Digital Art Projects

### What was it that you did?
I helped design concept art for a friend who was developing a video game. This game they 
were developing was a horror game, and they needed some concept art for an enemy they 
were thinking about adding. I designed a couple of versions of what the enemy would look 
like. I was also paid for this commission, which was nice.

### When did you do it?
I did this during the summer; it was a co-worker at Toyota who worked in a different area 
than I did. We eventually started talking, and I told them I had skills in art and design, 
and they asked if I wanted to help them design something.

### What was good about it?
Great experience, no pressure to meet a deadline, as they were pretty early in the game 
design. I had never done concept art before, so applying my skills into something more 
formatted was cool. 

### What was your favourite memory of it?
Drawing a character design from different angles. It let me work on my proportions and 
perspective a bit more. 

### What didn't you enjoy about it?
I didn't really dislike much about it. Though, because we worked in different areas, we 
mostly used Discord to communicate. Since they weren't in a rush to get the concept art, 
it took them quite a while to respond when I gave them updates on it.

### Give your experience a letter "grade"
A
