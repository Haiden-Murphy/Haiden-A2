Experience 5: Mohawk College


What was it that you did?

I followed a co-op program out of high school that went into Mohawk College for software 
development/programming. I would have to go back and look up what it was actually called, but I 
completed the program with a certificate. 


When did you do it?

I did this right after high school, so that would have been 2022, post-pandemic. Many classes 
were still online, but some were in person, and my previous high school provided transportation 
to make sure we got to those classes.


What was good about it?

I was in the program with my high school friends who took the same co-op classes as me. That was 
probably one of the best parts; we studied together and helped each other out. Also, it was a 
relatively short program that introduced me to secondary school.


What was your favourite memory of it?

Making friends with some of my professors; one professor was very helpful and understanding, and 
I loved that class. Also, hanging out with my friends between classes and finding our classes together 
was fun and not as stressful because we were all in the same boat.


What didn't you enjoy about it?

There were good professors, but there were also horrible professors, most notably one I disliked very 
much because of how they treated their students and how hard they were when grading. I find it's always 
a gamble, but there were more good ones than bad.


Give your experience a letter "grade"

B
